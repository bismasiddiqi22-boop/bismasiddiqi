MY STORY

About Bisma

I've spent my career at the intersection of requirements and execution. Translating fuzzy business asks into specs engineering can actually build, and specs back into outcomes stakeholders can actually see.

My process starts with the "why." Before a single user story gets written, I want to understand the constraint, the user, and the business case behind it.

I own the full lifecycle — from discovery and requirement gathering through prototyping, delivery, and QA. I like being the person a team can hand ambiguity to.

Along the way I've worked across AI/EdTech, maritime operations, health tech, and healthcare marketplaces — each one starting the same way: a messy set of requirements, and a product people actually needed.
