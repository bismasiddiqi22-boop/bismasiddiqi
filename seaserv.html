SeaServ

A connected digital ecosystem for luxury yacht operations — rebuilt from the ground up for guests, crew, chiefs, and management teams, after the original platform fell short of the premium experience the industry expects.

MY ROLE

Discovery, Architecture & Delivery

The challenge

SeaServ already had a digital platform — the problem wasn't that it didn't exist, it was that it didn't hold up. Fragmented workflows, inconsistent experiences, and a limited technical foundation made daily operations inefficient for guests, crew, and yacht management alike, in an industry where "good enough" isn't good enough.

Underneath the outdated visual design sat a deeper problem: no scalable design system, features added without a clear product architecture, and almost no real differentiation between four very different users — Guest, Crew, Chief, and Admin — who each needed the platform to do something different.

My role

The instinct with a project like this is to jump straight to a visual redesign. I pushed the other way: before touching a single screen, I worked to understand the client's vision for the business, then audited the existing platform end to end — mapping every workflow across all four user roles to understand exactly where and why it was breaking down.

That audit became the basis for a new product architecture, built from the ground up rather than patched on top of the old one. I coordinated across design and engineering to keep UX strategy, interface design, backend development, and QA moving as one connected process rather than a handoff chain — and stayed on through launch support to make sure what shipped matched what we'd scoped.

What we built

  • Guest mobile application — a dedicated, premium experience for the people the whole platform exists to serve.
  • Crew mobile application — workflows built around how crew actually operate day to day, not adapted from the guest experience.
  • Chief dashboard — oversight tools suited to a chief's operational responsibilities.
  • Super admin portal — centralized control for yacht management teams.
  • A unified design system and brand refresh — so all four surfaces feel like one product, not four.
  • A new product architecture — built on a Flutter frontend and Node.js backend, designed to scale.

Outcomes

  • A premium user experience aligned with luxury maritime standards, across every role on the platform.
  • Streamlined, role-specific workflows replacing the one-size-fits-all approach of the original product.
  • A scalable architecture built to support future product growth rather than more workarounds.
  • A consistent experience across web and mobile, backed by one unified design and engineering process.
  • A production-ready platform replacing the early-stage product it grew out of.

Built with

Design in Figma; Flutter on the frontend; Node.js on the backend — delivered end-to-end from UX strategy through backend engineering, QA, and launch support.
