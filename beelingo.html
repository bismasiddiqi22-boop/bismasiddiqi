MY ROLE

Business Analysis & Product Strategy

The challenge

Schools and educators were managing learning, assignments, assessments, and examinations across disconnected tools and manual processes. Beelingo set out to bring all of it into one scalable platform — and to use AI to remove real manual effort from academic content creation, not just bolt AI on as a feature.

The hard part wasn't the AI. It was the ecosystem underneath it: four very different user groups (schools, teachers, students, parents), spanning every grade, subject, and curriculum, all needing a coherent product rather than four disconnected ones.

My role

I led this engagement as Business Analyst and Product Manager on behalf of Dot2Shape, the digital product studio that owns and delivered the Beelingo platform. I led requirements gathering and stakeholder discovery across every user group — mapping how schools, teachers, students, and parents actually worked day to day, and translating that into structured user flows Dot2Shape's design and engineering teams could build against.

A large part of the job was keeping the product architecture honest as the scope grew: making sure a feature built for one grade or subject wouldn't need to be rebuilt when the next one was added. I worked across product strategy, UX direction, and architecture to keep the whole system modular rather than letting it sprawl.

The AI assignment generator needed the most careful requirements work. I defined what inputs it needed to take (subject, grade, topic, difficulty, learning objective) and — just as important — specified the human validation workflow sitting around it, so AI-generated assessments couldn't reach a classroom without a review step confirming accuracy and curriculum alignment.

What we built

  • Parent & student experience — access to learning activities, assignments, sessions, examinations, and academic progress in one place.
  • School & teacher experience — tools to manage learning content, assignments, assessments, and student activity.
  • AI assignment generator — generates questions based on subject, grade, topic, difficulty, and learning requirements.
  • Assessment & examination workflows — structured assessment and exam delivery, combining AI-generated content with human validation for accuracy and quality.
  • A modular foundation — built to expand across new subjects, grades, and academic workflows without re-architecting.

Outcomes

  • Simplified education and assessment workflows for every user group on the platform.
  • Reduced manual effort in academic content creation through AI-assisted generation with human oversight.
  • Supported multiple user types, grades, and subjects on one coherent platform, not four separate ones.
  • Left the team with a scalable foundation for AI-powered learning and assessment going forward.

Built with
